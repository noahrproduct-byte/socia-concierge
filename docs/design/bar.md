# The bar: raycast.com (homepage), read 2026-10-27

Seven mechanisms a critic can check by looking at a screenshot or a short scroll. Measured on the live page at 1440x900, not the Refero sheet.

1. **One statement above the fold, most of the frame empty.** The first screen holds the nav, a two-line headline, one button and one mono meta line. More than half of the hero's area is bare canvas. Nothing else is visible without scrolling.
2. **Colour appears twice per screen at most.** The page is white and grey on #040506. The brand hue is on the logo and inside one product artwork per screen, never on a button, a link, a border or body text.
3. **The product is the imagery.** Every visual below the hero is the app's own window, reconstructed at near container width (900px+), with a 1px hairline and an inset top highlight. No photographs of people, no stock, no illustration standing in for the product.
4. **Motion reveals once and resolves in one direction.** Elements fade in with a rise of 24px or less as they enter, in 200 to 500ms, then stay. Nothing loops, nothing bounces, nothing moves while the reader is reading. Demos inside windows respond to the cursor or a click; they do not autoplay.
5. **Four type sizes per screen, mono for metadata.** An uppercase mono eyebrow (11 to 12px), body at 16px, a subhead at 20 to 24px, and one display size (32 to 64px). Headings sit at weight 500 to 600, body at 400. Version strings, shortcuts and meta lines are mono with pipe separators.
6. **One idea per section, separated by canvas.** Each section has one heading and one visual. Sections are 100px or more apart with no alternating light bands. A heading is centred when its visual is centred, left-aligned when the visual sits beside it.
7. **Depth without drop shadows.** Cards and windows are defined by a 1px #363739 border or the inset "key" highlight, buttons are light fills on dark. No blurred shadow under any card, no glow around any control.
