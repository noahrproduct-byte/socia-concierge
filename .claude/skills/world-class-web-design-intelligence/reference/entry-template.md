Copy into `library/references/<slug>.md`. Write in your own words. Every bullet says what it does, why it works, and whether it transfers. Mark each as `[measured]`, `[observed]` or `[inferred]`.

- **Spacing and density:** base unit; row heights; content width.
- **Motion:** durations; easing; what animates.
- **Icons:** style; stroke; sizes per context; active state.
