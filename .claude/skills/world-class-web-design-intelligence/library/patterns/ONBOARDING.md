# ONBOARDING

## Approaches seen
- **EnduroSat** [observed]:
  - Progressive commitment: one easy question per screen (domain, then application), then a dense workspace.
  - Chapter progress shows what's done, current and ahead.
- **ElevenLabs** [observed]:
  - Real value is previewable before the account wall (voices play publicly).
  - Sign-up is asked for only at the first costly step.
  - Google sign-in comes first.
- **Higgsfield** [observed]: the empty state is the tutorial (three numbered steps with real examples).
- **Metricool** [observed]:
  - A concept explainer where the concept first matters ("What's a brand?").
  - Account-type choices explained at connect time.

## Recommendation for SOCIA
- **Connect flow:** progressive commitment (connect one account, then see real data), with a compressing progress indicator.
- **Explainers** for "Brand Workspace" and the Instagram connection types, placed where they first matter.
- **Gate at the first costly step, not at the door,** but only with honest public data. Otherwise use clearly labelled real examples, never fake numbers.
- **First-run screens** use numbered how-to empty states.

## Added from the logged-in apps (2026-10-19)
- **Metricool:** one feature-intro template for every unexplored module: a title, one paragraph, the primary action, then three benefit tiles with "Learn more". A "What's new" modal on sign-in, built by a third-party script. [observed]
- **ElevenLabs Home:** two ways in. A prompt bar (describe what you want), or mode tiles (pick the tool). A cross-tool Recents list makes "continue where I was" one click. [measured]
- **Higgsfield:** empty states teach the concept with a preview of the filled state; a three-step strip appears only where a concept needs teaching. [observed]
