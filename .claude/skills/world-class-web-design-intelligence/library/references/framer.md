## Measured system
- **Type (framer.com):** GT Walsheim Medium 500 for display only, Inter / Inter Variable 400–600 for everything else [measured]. At desktop, H1 is 54/54 and H2 is 44/48.4; feature titles (H3) are just 18/24.3 at **weight 400** [measured]. Tablet steps down to 42/36, phone to 36/28 [measured]. Pricing: 342 of 347 text nodes are weight 400 [measured]. Body 14/19.6 (×283 on pricing), then 16, 18, 24 [measured]. Tracking is negative and scales with size: about -0.04em on display (54px at -2.16px), -0.2px at 18px, -0.1px at 14px [measured]. Numbers in product mocks use Inter `tnum` plus character variants `cv01`/`cv09` [measured].
- **Type (marketplace):** Inter at weight 450 for 147 of 148 text nodes; 13px dominant, 12px secondary; H1 30px Walsheim [measured]. Tokenised sizes: 10/11/12/13/15/16/20/28/30/40 [measured]. A token `--uc-font-features-tabular` adds `tnum` to the house feature set [measured].
- **Colour:** pure black page; text as white at 100%, 80%, 60% and 40% (60% is the most common text colour: ×95 on home, ×192 on pricing) [measured]. The marketplace names the same ramp as tokens: text `#fff`, text-80 `#ccc`, text-60 `#999`, text-40 `#666`, cards `#171717`, fields `#242424`/hover `#2b2b2b`, lines `#212121`, soft border `#333` [measured]. Accent `#09f` blue, used for the billing toggle "on" state, live dots and the marketplace accent button. A purple `#9869fd` appears only inside a product mock (the WINNER label) [measured]. There are no status colours outside the mocks.
- **Shape:** framer.com radii 8px ×110, 15px ×46, 18, 20, 25px (outer bento container) [measured]. The marketplace scale is xs 5 / sm 8 / md 16 / dialog 18 / lg 20 / xl 30 / full [measured]. Almost no shadows: `0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,.1)` and 1px inset white 8–10% hairlines [measured]. The menu shadow is `0 2px 8px 10%` plus `0 1px 0 4%` [measured]. The only coloured shadow is a faint accent-tinted one on the accent button [measured]. The agents page rail uses `corner-shape: superellipse(1.5)` squircles [measured].
- **Spacing and density:** 1200px content width at 1440 (120px margins); 20px gutters on phones [measured]. Marketplace spacing runs on a **5px base** (5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, 50, 60, 80, 100) [measured]. Pricing compare rows are 99px tall; analytics-mock list rows 30px; marketplace controls 30px [measured].
- **Motion (framer.com, all JS, zero CSS transitions):** appear effects animate opacity only, as a spring (bounce 0.2, 0.4s), compiled into 41 linear keyframes that overshoot to 1.014 [measured]. Springs across the page modules: bounce .2/0.4s ×26, bounce 0/1s ×20, bounce .2/1s ×9, stiffness 500/damping 60 ×6, plus single uses of 0.1–0.6s springs [measured]. Tweens are rare: ease-out `[.12,.23,.5,1]` at 0.2–1s, ease-in-out `[.44,0,.56,1]` at 0.3s [measured]. Scroll reveals trigger at 50% visibility and most replay every time (`animateOnce: false`) [measured]. Animated illustrations pause off-screen and replay on view [measured].
- **Motion (marketplace, CSS):** duration tokens instant 0 / fast .1s / base .15s / slow .2s; easing tokens out `(.4,0,.2,1)`, popup `(0,0,.2,1)`, bounce `(.175,.885,.32,1.275)` [measured]. Colour and background change at 0.15s; thumbnail transform at 0.14s [measured].
- **Icons:** custom SVG sprites with stroke and fill set by CSS variables. They're **duotone**: a 2px white stroke over a white 20% secondary fill [measured]. Sizes: 16px ×17 on home, 12px checkmarks in plan cards, 17px in the compare table, 18px in the agents rail [measured]. The active rail icon is opacity 1, inactive ones 0.5 [measured]. Mega-menu items carry no icons [measured].

## Strongest design qualities
1. **Product UI shown as live DOM, not screenshots.** The CMS, Collaborate and Analytics pages rebuild editor chrome in real HTML: collection lists with counts, field tables with Live/Draft statuses, branch lists with relative times, comment threads with reply counts. The CMS mock's date field showed **today's date** (10/19/2026) [measured]. Agent text is split into per-character spans for a typing reveal [measured]. **Why it works:** real text stays sharp at any pixel density, matches the page's type and theme, and can be animated part by part. A current date and believable data signal "this is the real product, today" more than a polished image does. **For SOCIA:** sociaos.com's product imagery could be rendered from SOCIA's own components with fixture data (the `zz-*` fixture-preview trick), so marketing never drifts from the app. Keep the data honest and labelled as an example.
2. **Hierarchy from size and text opacity, almost never weight.** Two families, about six sizes, weight 400 nearly everywhere; emphasis comes from white at 100/80/60/40% [measured]. **Why it works:** with weight held constant, every step in size or contrast reads clearly. Contrast steps are simple to apply consistently and survive theme changes. **For SOCIA:** this targets the baseline's known problems (eight weights from 500 to 800, ten sizes between 9 and 13.5px). SOCIA is denser, so a fair adaptation is 3 weights (400/600/700) and the existing primary/secondary/muted text tokens doing the work 650/750/780 do now. Change it system-wide, never page by page.
3. **The comparison row shows the winner without hiding the loser.** In the A/B mock, the winning variant's row is full white, the other is `#999`, a tiny 10/600 accent label marks the winner, and the baseline's lift cell is "—" [measured]. **Why it works:** contrast does the ranking before anyone reads a number; keeping the losing row visible preserves context; "—" says "not applicable" instead of a misleading 0%. **For SOCIA:** Trial Reels results, plan-loop outcomes and post comparisons in Analytics. It matches Noah's "unknown is — with a reason" rule.
4. **Ranked breakdowns with the bar inside the row.** The Analytics mock pairs a five-KPI row (12/500 label, 20/600 value) with Sources, Pages, Countries and Devices panels. Each panel is a list of 30px rows whose background is a white 8% bar sized to the value; a dimension switcher sits in the panel header; a 5px accent dot marks the live metric [measured]. **Why it works:** the bar lives where the eye already is (no separate chart to cross-reference), and one header control swaps the dimension without a new screen. **For SOCIA:** Analytics top posts, traffic sources, audience countries and devices. **But** the mock's bars aren't proportional (536K gets 442px, 88K gets 210px; linear would be about 72px) [measured]. SOCIA must scale linearly from zero.
5. **Usage is a control inside the plan, priced live.** Basic and Pro each carry a native `<select>` of monthly AI-credit tiers, and picking one updates the price at once (Basic went from CA$14 to CA$70 at 5,000 credits) [measured]. The compare table explains every row in one line under its label, puts overage pricing and caps inline (cost per extra unit plus a maximum), and groups rows into Limits, Agents, Live collaboration and Add-ons [observed]. **Why it works:** the person sizes the variable cost before checkout. A native select is keyboard- and phone-friendly for free. Inline overage terms answer "what happens if I exceed it?" in the row itself. **For SOCIA:** the cost model says Growth and Pro lose money at full AI use; a credit dimension per plan, shown this way, is one honest fix. It fits /pricing and Settings → Plan.
6. **On phones, the compare table becomes one plan at a time.** Below 810px the table shows a single plan column with a sticky plan switcher (41px tabs plus the price) [measured]. **Why it works:** a 3–4 column matrix can't be read at 375px; one column plus a persistent switcher keeps rows full width and the context visible. **For SOCIA:** the pricing page and any plan-compare sheet on mobile.
7. **The AI is taught through concrete, copyable prompts organised by job.** The agents page opens with ten job tiles (anchor links). Each job is a section with a sticky description on the left and prompt groups on the right; each prompt has a copy button; a sticky icon rail scroll-spies the jobs [measured]. **Why it works:** recognition beats recall. Seeing real requests sets expectations about scope and phrasing better than any capability list does. **For SOCIA:** Settings → AI Assistants (the MCP connection), with example prompts per job ("plan my week", "compare platforms") and a copy button. The same idea works as starter prompts in Generate Caption's empty state.
8. **The agent narrates its work, then hands over alternatives.** The design-agent mock shows the prompt, a thinking state, a completed step with elapsed time ("Created a design plan · 2s"), then the result placed as **named explorations** side by side [observed]. The homepage prompt box has starter chips and a visible "Start without AI" path [observed]. **Why it works:** step-plus-time feedback makes the wait legible and auditable, named variants turn one answer into a choice, and the manual path keeps AI optional. **For SOCIA:** Make This Video, Plan These Posts and Generate Caption: show the real steps (for example "Read your last 30 posts") with timing, offer 2–3 labelled options, and always keep the manual composer one click away.
9. **Async-dependent chrome fades in once it's known.** An inline auth check decides between Log in/Sign up and the signed-in state; those buttons then appear with the 0.4s opacity spring [measured]. A signed-in visitor gets a "D" shortcut to the dashboard, and the projects page is pre-rendered via speculation rules [measured]. **Why it works:** nothing flashes the wrong state, and the returning user's next step is instant. **For SOCIA:** the top bar's plan badge, workspace switcher and connection status should reserve their space and fade in on resolution, not flash a default. sociaos.com could show "Open SOCIA" and pre-render /dashboard when a session exists.

## Best components
- **Mega-menu drawer:** full width, height fitted to its content (128–197px), opaque black with a 1px hairline, a 60% black scrim over the page [measured]. Three to four columns headed by small 14px/60% labels; the Platform groups are ordered as a journey (Create, Build, Grow). Items are 18px/400 text with no icons or descriptions, plus one featured image card on the right [measured]. The nav bar is opaque black, with no blur or glass [measured].
- **Logo menu:** a secondary action on the logo offers "Copy logo SVG" and "Brand guidelines" [observed]. It's cheap and useful for press.
- **Pricing sticky header:** plan name + price at 16px stays pinned while 99px rows scroll [measured].
- **Marketplace grid tile:** 4:3 thumbnail at 285px in a 4-column grid with a 20px gap; name 13px white, creator 13px `#999`, price right-aligned at 12px [measured]. Sort is a segmented control with `aria-pressed` (active: `#1f1f1f` fill and white text; inactive: transparent and `#999`). Filters are 30px menu buttons with `aria-haspopup="menu"` [measured].
- **Category directory:** nine columns, each a white parent link with four `#999` child links on a 25px pitch [measured]. A whole taxonomy fits in 118px of height.
- **Menu with shortcut hints:** a marketplace menu item shows its keyboard shortcut (⌃⌘N) right-aligned [observed].
- **Roles as capabilities:** the Collaborate mock shows a person with role chips, and each role maps to what it unlocks (Design → canvas; Content → CMS and on-page editing; Deploy → publish) [observed].
- **Branch review:** a branch list with last-activity times, a change count, a list of affected pages and components, then Merge/Apply [observed].

## Best interactions
- **Focus equals hover.** Marketplace tiles lift -3px on keyboard focus through `:has(:focus-visible)`, the same feedback as pointer hover, at 0.14s ease-out [measured]. Keyboard users get the same signal.
- **Opacity-only appear for chrome.** Nav buttons fade without moving (0.4s spring with a 1.4% overshoot that's invisible on opacity) [measured]. Nothing jumps.
- **Off-screen animations pause** and replay when they come back into view [measured]. Motion costs nothing while unseen.
- **Live price recalculation** on credit-tier change [measured].
- **Forms survive early submits.** An inline script queues a submit made before JavaScript hydrates, and the prompt form carries hidden honeypot inputs [measured]. Fast typists don't lose input, and bots are filtered without a CAPTCHA.
- **Typewriter placeholder** in the prompt box (it was mid-word when sampled) [measured]. Decorative; see below.

## Best UX patterns
- **Job-first information architecture** on the AI page and in the Solutions menu (grouped by role) [observed]. People find capabilities by what they want to do.
- **Inline plan economics:** viewers are free, editor seats are priced per editor, the overage rule (one grace month plus an email) sits in the FAQ, and each limit row carries its own overage terms [observed].
- **Explicit escape hatch from AI** ("Start without AI") [observed].
- **Status in the footer:** a quiet "All services online" indicator [observed].

## What makes it feel premium
- **Restraint:** pure black, one ramp of white opacities, and the accent almost absent from the chrome, so the product mocks carry the only colour [measured].
- **Size-scaled negative tracking** (about -0.04em on display, nearly 0 on body) and Inter character variants on figures [measured].
- **Physical motion with little bounce:** springs at bounce 0–0.2 instead of linear or default ease; opacity-only for chrome, movement only for content [measured].
- **Hairlines instead of shadows:** inset 1px white 8–10% borders and almost no drop shadows [measured].
- **Real, current data in the mocks** (today's date, believable counts and statuses) [measured].

## Weaknesses
- **Accessibility gaps on custom controls:** the billing toggle is a `div` with `tabindex` but no switch role or checked state; the mobile plan tabs have no tab roles; the copy-prompt buttons and the icon rail have no labels; the prompt's submit button (20×20) and the hamburger have no `aria-label` [measured].
- **Small touch targets:** hero CTAs are 29px tall on phones and the billing toggle is 20×12 [measured]. The hamburger is a good 64×64 [measured].
- **Prices swap after load:** server-rendered prices showed USD ($10/$30) and switched to CA$14/CA$41 after hydration [measured]. The likely result is a visible flash of the wrong price [inferred].
- **Mock data takes liberties:** the analytics bars aren't proportional to their values [measured].
- **Scroll-heavy pages:** the agents page runs about 19,000px of stacked sticky cards, and reveals replay on every pass [measured]. That's tiring to re-scan and makes the motion repetitive [inferred].
- **Weight-400 titles** scan weakly in dense lists [inferred]. That's fine on a marketing page, not in an app table.
- **Two visible systems:** framer.com (weight 400, radii 8/15/25, Framer runtime) and the marketplace (weight 450, its own token scale, its own header) [measured]. It's the same seam SOCIA has between sociaos.com and the app.
- **Consent mismatch:** the analytics product is sold on not needing cookie banners, while framer.com shows an Ok-only banner and loads about ten third-party tracking scripts on first view [measured from the script list].

## Patterns NOT relevant to SOCIA
- **Giant display type** (54px Walsheim with -2.16px tracking) and full-black marketing theming: Noah's rules exclude giant type, and the app needs light and dark.
- **Bento feature grid** and **stacked sticky cards** across very long pages: bento grids are on Noah's do-not list, and long scroll-driven stories don't fit a dense working app.
- **Reveals that replay on every scroll pass,** the hero video with a grain overlay, customer-site marquees and the typewriter placeholder: decoration with no state meaning.
- **Duotone icon sprites and superellipse corners:** they'd break lucide consistency; `corner-shape` is a Chromium-only nicety.
- **A full-width mega-menu inside the app:** SOCIA's navigation is its sidebar.

## Do not copy
- The Framer logo, the GT Walsheim typeface (licensed), the black-plus-`#09f` identity, the customer logos and stories, template thumbnails, the hero video and its grain treatment.
- Their marketing copy, taglines and headlines (paraphrase lessons only), and their example prompt texts.
- The signature layouts as wholes: the agents page's tiles-plus-rail-plus-stacked-cards composition, the bento grid, and the mega-menu with its featured image card.
