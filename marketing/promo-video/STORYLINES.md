# SOCIA promo: three storylines

Reference: the creator reel (54 s, 9:16, six chapters, blur-dissolve seams, two-beat titles, element-by-element builds, one takeaway line per chapter). What works in it: one spine, a real number in the hook, every diagram built in reading order, a takeaway line before each seam. What is weak: invented-looking watch list, orange glow on everything, every chapter entering the same way, the diagram floats in a void, no product on screen.

Every number below is real and was read from SOCIA's own MCP tools for the Salvo's Pizza workspace (@salvoshermitage) on 2026-10-27. Nothing is illustrative unless labelled.

## A. Monday, 8:12 AM (recommended)

**Device:** a moment in someone's day. **Form:** object-to-object chain: every shot ends on the thing that opens the next.

A pizzeria owner opens SOCIA on a Monday. The Today card says a post failed, nothing is scheduled, and the next strong window is Tuesday 9 AM to 12 PM. One real post, "Perfection." (102K views, 85x the account's median), travels through the whole film: it is the outlier SOCIA finds, the pattern the plan repeats, the clip Content Studio cuts next, the answer Ask SOCIA gives, the post the Calendar schedules for Tuesday, and the insight Claude reads through MCP. The viewer feels: it already knows what I should do.

- **First two seconds:** a phone-sized SOCIA screen, dark, the Today card already on it: "A post didn't publish", "Nothing is set to publish in the next 3 days". Over it, one line: "Social media moves fast."
- **Beats:** hook (the Today card) → tension ("Most people are still guessing.") → reveal (the mark, "Know what to do next.") → connect (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube; TikTok marked not connected) → analytics (49.8K views, 77K reach, 9 posts, median 27) → outliers ("Perfection." 85x) → audience and niche (formats: 48 of 50 are reels; timing: Tuesday +389%; local posts vs the rest) → competitors (the Competitors page: real tracked accounts, observed numbers) → content plan (the seven real posts) → Content Studio (the real clips, "From dough to box" ready) → reels tracking (views, reach, saves, shares per post) → Ask SOCIA ("What should I post this week?") → publishing (the Calendar, Tuesday 9:15 AM, Instagram and Facebook) → MCP (Claude reading the same workspace) → claim ("Stop guessing. Start knowing.") → end card.
- **Look:** the homepage's world. Near-black ground, warm white Inter Tight, gold for the one word that matters in a frame; the product in its own dark app surfaces with cobalt only where the app uses it; Salvo's real footage as the only colour.
- **Sound:** a quiet, steady bed that opens at the reveal and lifts at the plan; a soft tick as each row of a list lands; one riser into the end card. (No music source is available in this environment, so it is built silent; the bed slots in as one `<audio>`.)

## B. Guessing, knowing

**Device:** life before and after. **Form:** split screen to one.

The frame is split: the left half churns (a blank caption field, a posting time picked by scrolling, a competitor's reel watched for the fifth time); the right half is calm (SOCIA's Today card, the plan, the scheduled post). Each feature is one left-right pair; at the claim the left half collapses and the right half fills the frame. The viewer feels the relief of the right side.

- **First two seconds:** both halves live. Left: a cursor hovering a blank caption. Right: "Post at Tuesday 9 AM, 389% above your typical post."
- **Beats:** pair ×9 (one per feature) → collapse → claim → end card.
- **Look:** the left half on paper (#f2efe9) in grey, the right half on black in warm white and gold; the seam is a 2 px rule.
- **Sound:** the left half near silence with UI clicks; the bed lives only on the right and takes the whole mix at the collapse.

## C. Type carries the argument

**Device:** type carries the argument. **Form:** cut on the beat.

Giant Inter Tight lines, one idea each, cut hard on a 120 BPM grid; every third bar a real product screen punches in as evidence and leaves. Fastest and boldest; weakest at explaining what the product does, which the brief asks for.

- **First two seconds:** "Social media moves fast." fills the frame, each word a beat.
- **Beats:** eight lines → eight evidence punches → claim → end card.
- **Look:** black and paper alternating per bar, type at 9% of the frame height.
- **Sound:** the music is the clock; cuts on the beat.

## Pick

A, because a stranger follows one owner and one post from Monday morning to a scheduled Tuesday, and every screen is the real product doing its job. The brief asks for every feature to be explained, which A carries as steps of one task and C cannot.
