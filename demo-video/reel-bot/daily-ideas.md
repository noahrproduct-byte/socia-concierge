# Daily reel bank

What the daily reels can be about, and how they can be told. Every claim must be
true of SOCIA today; when unsure, check the app or the memory notes, never guess.
Add new true features here as they ship.

## True things SOCIA does

- Reads Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok accounts in one place (TikTok: account
  snapshot and per-video totals only).
- Finds the posts that beat your own average, and shows what they have in common.
- Builds a weekly content plan from your own results; every post lands as a draft.
- Nothing posts until you approve it. Team members submit; owners and admins approve.
- Today card: a short list of what to do today, from your numbers.
- "Worth posting again": older posts that did well and are worth a repost.
- Trial Reels on Instagram: test a reel with non-followers first.
- Writes captions from what it actually knows about your account, and checks them for
  claims it can't back up.
- Competitors and trending accounts or reels in your niche: copy the idea, not the post.
- Content Studio rater: scores a reel's hook, pacing, clarity and CTA, and says what to fix.
- Ask SOCIA: tell it to schedule or move posts in plain words; it confirms first.
- Footage library: drop your clips in, plan them across the week.
- Brand workspaces: one place per brand or client, kept apart.
- Connects to Claude (and other AI assistants): Claude reads your SOCIA data and
  builds the video, then SOCIA rates it, publishes it when you approve, and tracks it.
- Deletes a post from Instagram, Facebook or YouTube for you, after confirming.

## Formats (rotate; never the same one twice in a row)

1. Myth vs fact: three creator myths, each busted by what the data actually shows.
2. POV skit: "POV: you finally know what to post", a creator's morning, told in captions.
3. Screen-tour speedrun: one feature, start to finish, in 20 seconds.
4. "I asked Claude to..." chat reel: a Claude chat window does the job on SOCIA data.
5. Before and after: a messy week of guessing vs a planned week (labelled EXAMPLE).
6. Three mistakes: what kills reach, and what SOCIA flags instead.
7. Kinetic type manifesto: bold words only, voice and motion, about creating with less guessing.
8. Countdown: "5 things your analytics app should tell you (most don't)".
9. Comment reply: answer a question a creator might ask (make the question a plain
   example, never a fake real comment with a name or avatar of a real person).
10. Hot take: one strong opinion about posting, backed by how SOCIA works.
11. Tiny tutorial: "how to find your best posting time in 10 seconds".
12. Mascot moment: the SOCIA agent character (src/agent) in a new, funny situation.

## Hooks that work (first second)

A bold claim on screen, a question aimed at "you", a surprising visual, or a pattern
interrupt. Never a fake number.
